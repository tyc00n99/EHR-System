Every visit verified. Every claim paid. One system.

Generic home-care platforms were built for other states and other programs. EVVora was built for Minnesota 245D, and it shows in three places.

1

Verification you never think about

Every visit is GPS-checked at both ends and held against the state's EVV rules while it is happening, not at month end. Problems surface in your review queue while they can still be fixed, instead of after a claim bounces.

2

Billing that flows into MNITS

A signed note already carries its claim line, checked against the authorization before anything leaves. Claims go through Waystar into MNITS, and remittances come back onto the claim, so you know what is paid and what is stuck.

3

Paperwork that reads itself

Hand EVVora the county's service agreement letter, a referral packet or a training certificate. It reads the document, fills in the form, and you confirm what it found. Nothing gets retyped, and every filed document becomes searchable.

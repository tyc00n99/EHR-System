How a visit works

Clock in. Do the visit. Sign. Done.

The phone is the time clock. The note is a few fields, not a form.

1

Clock in

Only for assigned, oriented clients. The scheduled event links itself.

2

Write the note

Level of assistance, supports, activities and the outcomes addressed.

3

Sign together

The client enters their code, the caregiver signs, and the note is accepted.

The note

Prints the way a reviewer reads it.

One page per visit. Client, date, code, hours and units at the top; both signatures at the foot.

  • Daily service note, one per visit
  • Biweekly summary per caregiver and service
  • PDF for any client, range or pay period

    Questions

    Questions agencies actually ask

    Is it built for 245D, or adapted from something else?

    Built for it. Service codes and modifiers come from the DHS list, units are 15 minutes, planning deadlines and the personnel file follow what a 245D licensor looks for, and the client identifier is the PMI.

    Does it send visits to the state's EVV aggregator?

    Every visit is recorded and shaped for the HHAeXchange aggregator, with location at clock-in and clock-out and a reason on any manual entry. Submission turns on once your agency's aggregator connection is set up; until then nothing is sent.

    Can office staff sign a note for a client or caregiver?

    No. The client signs with their own code and the caregiver signs their own note. A supervisor can enter a missed visit by hand, but both signatures still wait for the people themselves, and the note stays on hold until they sign.

    Does EVVora run payroll?

    No. It adds up each caregiver's regular and overtime hours for the pay period, using your overtime rules and each person's pay rate history, so you can enter them in your payroll provider. Notes that are not signed yet are held back.

    What do caregivers need?

    A phone with a browser. They sign in, clock in at the visit, write the note, and hand the phone to the client to sign.

    How is our data protected?

    Staff Social Security numbers are encrypted, every change to a client, staff or visit record is logged with who made it, and files are only shown to people allowed to see that client.

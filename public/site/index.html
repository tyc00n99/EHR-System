How it works

Their job: clock in and write. Yours: review.

Caregivers do what they already do on shift. EVVora does the checking, the formatting and the paperwork behind it.

01 · CAREGIVER

Clock in at the visit

On their phone, with GPS at both ends. They can only clock in with clients they are assigned and oriented to.

Takes one tap
02 · CAREGIVER + CLIENT

Write the note together

The narrative, supports, outcomes and that day's medications, then the client signs with their own code.

Before they clock out
03 · YOU

Review, bill and pay

Notes that need you land in one queue. The claim lines and each caregiver's hours for the pay period are already added up.

As the visit happens

Each moment of the visit lands in the note the county reads, with where it came from beside it. By clock-out there is nothing left to type up.

Why EVVora

Why agencies switch to EVVora.

Generic home-care platforms were built for other states and other programs. EVVora was built for Minnesota 245D — and it shows in three places.

EVV & compliance

Verification you never think about

Every visit is GPS-checked at clock-in and clock-out and held against the state's EVV rules as it happens, then queued for the aggregator. Problems surface in the review queue while they can still be fixed — not after the claim bounces.

Practice management

Billing that flows into MNITS

A signed note is already a claim line — units, codes and modifiers checked against the authorization — ready for clearinghouses integrated directly with Minnesota's MNITS. Less retyping, fewer rejections, faster payment.

Made for Minnesota

245D native, not adapted

PMI numbers, 15-minute units, the DHS service list, county case managers and the licensor's personnel-file checklist are the product, not a configuration project. Software built for another program has to be bent into shape; EVVora speaks 245D out of the box.

Billing & EVV compliance

From signed note to paid claim, without leaving EVVora.

Every signed, EVV-verified note is already a claim line. From there, EVVora moves it the rest of the way — through Waystar and into Minnesota's own payment system — so billing is something that happens, not something you do.

EVVora
YOUR RECORD

The claim line writes itself

Service code, modifiers and 15-minute units come straight off the signed note, checked against the authorization before anything leaves the building.

Waystar
CLEARINGHOUSE

Delivered through Waystar

Waystar scrubs each claim and submits it electronically — no payer portals, no rekeying, rejections caught before the state ever sees them.

MNITS
MINNESOTA DHS

Straight into the state's system

Claims land in MNITS, Minnesota's Medicaid payment system, and remittances flow back onto the claim in EVVora — so you always know what's paid, pending or denied.

EVV compliance rides the same rails. Every visit is checked against the state's EVV rules as it happens and sent to the HHAeXchange aggregator — so the claim and its verification always match, and nothing bills that wasn't verified.

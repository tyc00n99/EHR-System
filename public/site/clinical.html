Medication administration

A paper MAR, kept live.

The medication administration record the licensor expects — a week at a time, one row per medication and scheduled time — except nobody fills it in at the end of the month.

  • Recorded during the visit. The day’s doses appear on the caregiver’s phone at clock-in, next to the note they’re already writing.
  • Given, refused, held or missed — each mark carries a note and the initials of whoever recorded it, so “who gave the 8:30 dose” is never a mystery.
  • Month tallies per medication, counted against the doses actually due — the number a reviewer asks for first.
  • History stays honest. A medication changes or is discontinued in place; doses already recorded are the record, and can never be deleted.
